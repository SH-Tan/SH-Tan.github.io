I am a Ph.D. student at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, advised by Prof. Radoslav Ivanov. My research focuses on making neural networks and large language models more efficient and interpretable, with an emphasis on model pruning, knowledge distillation, and graph-based analysis.

My current work studies how to prune language models with their subsequent distillation in mind: which parts of a model can a teacher help recover? Previously, I worked on autonomous-driving perception and risk assessment at the University of Science and Technology of China.

Current research · LLM compression

Distillation-Aware Pruning for LLMs

I develop pruning methods that account for what a teacher can repair during distillation. DAP jointly models immediate pruning damage and recoverability to design stronger compressed students.

Experiments on Qwen3-8B and DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama-8B show gains of up to 3.9 percentage points in average post-distillation performance over the strongest pruning baseline, under matched sparsity and training budgets.

Manuscript under review at ICLR 2027.

Neural network compression · Graph geometry

Post-Training Pruning Using Graph Curvature

An interpretable pruning method that uses graph Ricci curvature to identify important information flows and redundant connections. My work includes GPU-accelerated curvature computation and extensions to transformer information-flow analysis.
